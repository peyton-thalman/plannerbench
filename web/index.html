PlannerBench

Can AI answer retirement questions correctly? I tested it.

PlannerBench is a set of retirement planning questions with known right answers. I give the questions to AI models and grade what they say.

The questions cover taxes, Social Security, Medicare, required minimum distributions, and Roth conversions under 2026 rules. I built a planning engine to work out the correct answers, and a second, separately written program to double-check them.

By Peyton Thalman, financial advisor in Sun City West, Arizona.

Why I built it

I’m a financial advisor, and I meet a lot of retirees by knocking doors. Many of them tell me they ask ChatGPT their money questions. When I ask how they know the answer is right, they say they Googled it and the top result agreed. That top result is usually Google’s AI. I wanted a real way to check, so I built one.

If you use AI for retirement questions

Check the ages, balances, and income it used. Ask which rule it’s applying. If it can’t tell you, or it got your facts wrong, don’t act on the answer. For decisions you can’t undo, like when to start Social Security, talk to a person.

For financial firms

Your clients are already asking AI before they ask you. Most can’t tell when it’s wrong, and the AI usually won’t tell them when the facts they gave it are wrong.
